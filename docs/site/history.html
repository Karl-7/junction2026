Haiku

  • Seed 101 (0.258 vs 0.790). Purity matched the reference (0.607 vs 0.630) but recovery was 0.100 against 0.322: every mother liquor was discarded. Its own report names the cause and does nothing about it: “each crystallisation step discarded ~30-50% of the target into mother liquor”. It submitted with 49 actions left.
  • Seed 102 (0.346 vs 0.939). Six crystallisations with the liquor discarded each time. It stopped because “recovery would fall faster than purity rises”, which is true only when liquors are thrown away; recycling is how to escape that trade-off.
  • Seed 103 (0.360 vs 0.862). It opened with an extreme fraction of 0.05, then abandoned its crystal branch and purified the old mother liquor in a second chain without ever combining the two. Its report calls this “repeated crystallisation of mother liquors”; the log shows zero combine calls.

Opus

  • Seed 101 (0.745 vs 0.790). Recycled, but spent 56 of 100 actions on instruments and only 15 on crystallisation, so purity stopped at 0.633. Its report: “I had too few actions to push purity above about 0.65”.
  • Seed 102 (0.987 vs 0.939). Modelled the process before acting and concluded that “a plain chain of crystallizations topped out around a 0.72 score”; recycling did much better. 8 recycle rounds, submitted on its 100th action.
  • Seed 103 (1.000 vs 0.862). Estimated the crystallisation odds ratio (about 7.7), re-planned each of 9 stages from fresh readings, and corrected its atomic weight to within 0.2% of the true value.

The recycling decision separates models: Haiku misses it on all three seeds, Opus finds it on all three. v1 therefore gives a large, explainable gap for a small model but none yet for a frontier-class model. Next levers: a reference that adapts its fractions to a measured odds ratio, and a tighter action budget.

Earlier versions

  • v0b. Haiku averaged 0.768 against 0.961. It used small crystallisation fractions (0.2–0.25) and on seed 101 recombined its final crystals with their mother liquor, undoing that round. Three Gemini 3.5 Flash runs are excluded: the free-tier key allows 20 requests per day, so none reached a submission.
  • v0a. Opus and Sonnet matched or beat the reference (0.917, 1.000, 0.969). Exploit testing then showed the carrier could be read off the public ore assay and that a microscopic pure sample earned report credit; both were fixed in v0b.
